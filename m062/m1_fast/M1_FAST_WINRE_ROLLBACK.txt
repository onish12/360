PHASER360 M1 FAST-SAFE - WINRE ROLLBACK

Acest pachet NU modifica BCD si NU reporneste automat sistemul.

Inainte de bind, runnerul creeaza M1_FAST_RECOVERY_POINTER.txt cu numele exact oemNN.inf al pachetului experimental.

Daca Windows nu mai porneste:
1. Intra in Windows Recovery Environment -> Command Prompt.
2. Identifica litera partitiei Windows; nu presupune ca este C:.
3. Citeste M1_FAST_RECOVERY_POINTER.txt din folderul din care ai rulat pachetul si noteaza CUSTOM_INF=oemNN.inf.
4. Verifica:
   dism /Image:<WINDOWS_VOLUME>:\ /Get-Drivers /Format:Table
5. Elimina NUMAI CUSTOM_INF:
   dism /Image:<WINDOWS_VOLUME>:\ /Remove-Driver /Driver:oemNN.inf
6. Reporneste numai dupa ce DISM confirma eliminarea.

Intel baseline asteptat de tranzactie:
Service=IntcAudioBus
PublishedInf=oem14.inf
Version=9.22.0.4832
Provider=Intel(R) Corporation

Nu elimina oem14.inf.
