PHASER360 H15O - staged pre-firmware power policy

Reproduce etapizat hda_dsp_ctrl_clock_power_gating(false), fara DSP write:
1 GCTL.CRST 0->1
2 PPCTL.GPROCEN 0->1
3 CGCTL.ADPSDCGE bit1 ->0
4 EM2.L1SEN bit13 ->0
5 PGCTL.ADSPPGD bit2 ->1
Snapshot DSP/HDA dupa fiecare etapa si CPA0 read-only.
Rollback: CGCTL, EM2, PGCTL, GPROCEN si GCTL la baseline exact.

Fara ADSPCS write, DMA, IRQ ownership, firmware, DSP boot sau playback.
Ruleaza RUN_H15O_TRANSACTION.cmd ca Administrator si trimite RESULT_H15O_TRANSACTION_*.zip.
