PHASER360 H15M - PP capability discovery

Scop:
- foloseste numai tranzitia HDA GCTL.CRST 0->1->0 deja validata de H15I;
- cu GCTL ready, citeste LLCH si parcurge bounded lista HDA extended capabilities;
- identifica Processing Pipe capability (ID 3);
- captureaza PPCTL si PPSTS;
- raporteaza explicit starea PPCTL.GPROCEN bit30 si PIE bit31;
- restaureaza GCTL exact la 0.

NU scrie PPCTL, EM2, DSP BAR sau PCI config.
NU foloseste DMA, IRQ ownership, firmware, DSP boot sau playback.

Pornire: RUN_H15M_TRANSACTION.cmd
Trimite RESULT_H15M_TRANSACTION_*.zip.
